# Challenge-03-Smart-Cart
About 
This repository contains my Smart Cart challenge project.
Problem 
A student is purchasing items from an online store. Read the purchase amount, customer type (1 = Student, 2 = Regular Customer),
and delivery distance in kilometres. Determine the discount and delivery charge using conditional statements. Display the purchase
amount, discount amount, amount after discount, delivery charge, and final payable amount.

Algorithm 

1. Start.
2. Read the purchase amount.
3. Read the customer type ("1" for Student, "2" for Regular Customer).
4. Read the delivery distance in kilometres.
5. Check the customer type and purchase amount to determine the discount percentage.
6. Calculate the discount amount.
7. Calculate the amount after discount.
8. Check the delivery distance to determine the delivery charge.
9. Calculate the final payable amount by adding the delivery charge to the amount after discount.
10. Display the purchase amount, discount amount, amount after discount, delivery charge, and final payable amount.
11. Stop.
